Market temperature

Market temperature

A card above "Adjust" on Home that shows whether the Korean and US markets are cold, normal or hot right now. It is not a call on tops or bottoms — it is a reference thermometer for "is this an extreme stretch compared with the last 10 years?" It is not investment advice, and the app never says buy or sell — only head back to the targets you set.

Works with: the sample · your own assets · from 3.5.0 (iPhone · iPad · Mac · Android)

How it is measured

For each gauge, the app looks at where today's value sits within the last 10 years (0–100), then averages by question. A country's temperature is the average of its questions, not of its gauges — so a question with more gauges does not weigh more. Below 33 is cold, above 66 is hot. When it has moved more than 6 points since three months ago, ↑ warming or ↓ cooling is added.

Sources: Yahoo Finance (indexes), FRED (VIX · high-yield spread), KOFIA FreeSIS (margin · deposits · forced sales · foreign ownership), Korea Securities Depository open data (US stock settlement). They change once a day; the app refreshes every 6 hours. For VIX and the high-yield spread the app shows the source and the 10-year rank instead of the raw number.

The Home card

  • One word per country — cold · normal · hot — and a three-cell bar.
  • If your risk share is more than 5 pp above target while the market is hot, or more than 5 pp below while it is cold, a line appears that opens the adjustment plan. Otherwise it says nothing.

Tap it — Korea · US · Yours

Korea · US give a one-line verdict, the temperature and index over 3 months · 1 year · 5 years (press and slide on the chart to see a day), and each question's three-month trend with its gauges in plain words.

Yours puts the market and your portfolio on the same date axis.

  1. Your return — your return from the same starting day (bold gold) and KOSPI · S&P 500 (dotted), with the chosen gauge (red line) laid over them. The red and blue bands behind are that gauge's hot and cold zones. Return is counted from unrealised gain, so money you add does not show up as profit. Switch to Size to see your total and the money you put in (dotted).
  2. Your mix — risk · cash · target, recorded once a day; it becomes a trend after a few days.
  3. What moves with your portfolio — the correlation (−1 to +1) between each gauge and your return, using week-to-week changes. Tap a row to chart that gauge above. It needs a few weeks of history, and it does not mean one causes the other.
