# Home

> **Works with:** the sample · your own assets

The first screen when you open the app. The aim is to read the state of your money in
a few seconds. Lists live on their own tabs, not here.

## What is on it

1. **Total assets** — the amount, today's change and your gain so far, on one card.
   Amounts show by default; to cover them, turn on Settings &gt; App &gt; **Hide amounts**.
   They blur, and show only while you press and hold.
2. **The class bar** — real estate · overseas ETFs · funds · savings · cash · stocks in one row.
   **See details** underneath opens the [portfolio report](portfolio-analysis.md).
3. **What to do now** — appears only when something has drifted from its target by more than
   your threshold (5 pp by default). **Go adjust** opens the [adjustment plan](allocation-rebalancing.md).
   **When there is nothing to adjust, the card does not appear at all.**
4. **Markets** — three cells. The exchange rate always keeps its place, and the first two follow
   whichever market is open — KOSPI · KOSDAQ while Korea trades, Nasdaq 100 · S&P 500 while the US does.
   **More** shows all eleven, treasury yields and oil included.
5. **News** — two recent headlines. Tap to read; **More** goes to the [news tab](news-reports.md).

The account list and the list of gaps live on the [Assets tab](asset-ranking.md).
The first screen does not repeat the same list.

## On Mac · iPad

With a wide screen the total stretches sideways and the **largest accounts** and
**target gaps** lists come back as cards. These are machines for seeing several things
at once, so moving between tabs costs more than it does on a phone.

## Home screen widget "Up next"

> iPhone · iPad · Android from 3.1.0, Mac from 3.2.0.

Puts the one thing to do next on your home screen. It checks these in order and shows the first that applies.

1. **Rebalancing round** — days to the next round of a split rebalancing (**D-4** · **Today**) and how many orders.
   If you have reserved orders at your broker, the day they go out comes first. With several accounts waiting, the
   soonest one is large and the rest are listed with their dates.
2. **Contribution day** — with the [contribution split](allocation-rebalancing.html) reminder on, it counts down from
   a week before.
3. **Holding furthest from target** — otherwise, the gap in percentage points and the target bar (gold is now, the
   dark tick is the target).

Tap it to go straight to that task — the rebalancing round, the contribution split or the account. It comes in small
and medium sizes; medium lists the orders or each account's date on the right.

- **No amounts or quantities.** Other people can see a home screen, so it shows names, weights and dates only.
- The widget never goes online by itself. It redraws when you open the app and counts the days down at midnight on its own.
- To add it: long-press the home screen and choose **Add widget** (Android: **Widgets**; Mac: right-click the desktop › **Edit Widgets**), then pick Golgoru.

## Worth knowing

- Dollar holdings are converted to won with the USD/KRW value from market indicators,
  **inside the app only**.
- Pull down to refresh prices, assets and news together.
  On Mac and iPad it is the refresh button at the top right.
- Appearance (system · light · dark) and text size are in Settings &gt; App.
